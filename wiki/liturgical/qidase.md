## What this page is

This is the **order of service (ሥርዓት) of the Divine Liturgy as actually celebrated at KMCC**, in the Ge'ez Catholic tradition, reconstructed from the parish's own liturgy slides. It is the *rite* — the sequence of texts the priest (ካህን / ይ.ካ.), deacon (ዲያቆን / ይ.ዲ.), and people (ሕዝብ / ይ.ሕ.) actually say and sing.

For the **doctrine** of the Mass — what it *is*, why it is a sacrifice, the real presence — see [[teaching/the-mass]]. For the **practical "what do I do at each part"** see [[teaching/practical-mass]]. This page sits between them: it documents the concrete Ge'ez-rite text, part by part, and points you to the exact slide in the raw source.

The source deck exists in two parallel versions — **Ge'ez** (277 slides, the sung liturgical language with Amharic rubrics) and **Amharic** (173 slides, the vernacular). Both are the same rite; the Ge'ez is longer because it carries more of the chanted propers.

## Which rite, which anaphora

This is a **Ge'ez Rite Catholic** liturgy — the ancient Ethiopian *Qiddase* structure, in full communion with Rome (see the Ge'ez vs. Latin Rite note in [[teaching/the-mass]]). Its signature is the **anaphora** system: the Ethiopian tradition has fourteen anaphoras (Eucharistic prayers), and a celebration selects one.

The KMCC deck carries **more than one anaphora** — the institution narrative, the Sanctus (ቅዱስ ቅዱስ ቅዱስ), and the epiclesis each appear in the deck more than once because it bundles alternatives the celebrant chooses between. Two are clearly present:

1. **The principal / ordinary anaphora** — the main Eucharistic prayer (Amharic slides 64–118; Ge'ez slides 123–262).
2. **The Anaphora of Our Lady (የእመቤታችን የቁርባን ጸሎት / ቅዳሴ ማርያም)** — the beloved Marian anaphora, explicitly titled in the deck (Amharic slide 138; Ge'ez slide 143).

Exactly which of the fourteen the "ordinary" one is (Apostles, Our Lord, John Chrysostom…) is not yet confirmed — see **Open questions**.

## Key texts (verbatim from the source)

**The institution narrative — the Body** (Amharic slide 79):

> ወደ ሰማይ፤ ወደ አንተ፤ ወደ አባቱ ራሱን ቀና አደረገ አመሰገነ ባረከ ቀደሰ ቆርሶም ለደቀመዛሙርቱ ሰጣቸው እንዲህም አላቸው እንካችሁ ብሉ ለኃጢያት ስሬት ስለናንተ የሚፈተት ይህ ሥጋዬ ነው።

The people's answer (slide 80):

> አሜን አሜን አሜን እናምናለን … ይህ ሥጋህ መሆኑን በውነት እናምናለን፡

**The institution narrative — the Blood** (Amharic slide 81):

> እንዲሁም ጽዋውንም አመስግኖ ባርኮ ቀድሶ ለደቀመዛርቱ ሰጣቸው እንዲህም አላቸው ለብዙዎች ቤዛ ስለናንተ የሚፈስ ይህ ጽዋ ደሜ ነው፡፡

These are the words of consecration — the same *"this is my body / this is my blood"* the whole Church has prayed since the Last Supper (cf. [[teaching/the-mass]], on the one sacrifice re-presented).

## The Marian character

Ethiopian eucharistic piety is deeply Marian, and this deck shows it: the salutation to Our Lady near the opening (ሰአሊ ለነ ድንግል ማርያም ወላዲተ አምላክ), and above all the full **Anaphora of Our Lady** (slide 138 / 143), which frames the offering with praise of the Theotokos:

> ልቤ መልካም ነገርን ያፈልቃል፤ እኔም የድንግልን ምስጋናና ትልቅነትዋን እናገራለሁ …
> — Amharic slide 139 (opening of the Anaphora of Our Lady)

On the doctrine behind this, see [[figures/ማርያም]] and the Marian teaching pages; on how it compares to EOTC/Orthodox eucharistic practice, see [[comparative/eucharist]].
